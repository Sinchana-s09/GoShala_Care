Emergency Bovine First-Aid Protocols

Critical actions to stabilize cattle while waiting for doctor arrival
Acute Bloat (Severe Left Flank Distension & Asphyxia)
  • Keep head elevated: Prop up cow's front quarters with straw to ease pressure on the diaphragm and prevent suffocation.
  • Place a wooden gag in mouth: Secure a 2-inch wooden stick across the mouth (like a horse bit) to stimulate chewing, salivation, and belching.
  • Administer anti-frothing oil: Drench 300–500 ml of vegetable oil (mustard, peanut, or linseed oil) or liquid paraffin.
Downer Cow / Post-Calving Milk Fever (Hypocalcemia)
  • Prop cow into sternal position: Use straw bales to keep cow resting on chest, NOT flat on side (prevents fatal bloat).
  • DO NOT force-feed oral liquids: Swallowing reflex is paralyzed; liquids cause fatal pulmonary aspiration.
  • Warm extremities: Wrap cold ears and legs with dry gunny sacks. Requires IV Calcium Borogluconate from veterinarian.
Foot & Mouth Sores (Blisters & Salivation)
  • Isolate immediately: Separate cow to shaded dry pen. Highly contagious.
  • Mouth wash: Gently wash mouth with 1% Potassium Permanganate or 2% alum water twice daily.
  • Hoof hygiene: Apply antiseptic zinc oxide paste to clefts to prevent secondary fly maggot strikes.
